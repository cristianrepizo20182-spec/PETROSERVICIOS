Our History

Track record and purpose

Photo / timeline

Petroservicios Industriales S.A.S. provides metalworking and industrial services for the oil & gas, industrial, mining and energy sectors: manufacturing, inspection, maintenance, repair and remanufacturing of tools, accessories, parts and equipment.

Mission

Through the supply of metalworking and industrial services, PSI seeks to meet the needs of the oil & gas, industrial, mining and energy sectors in relation to the manufacturing, inspection, maintenance, repair and remanufacturing of tools, accessories, parts or equipment, managing the business in a way that generates value for the company and its stakeholders.

Vision

By 2027, PSI will be recognized for the quality of its products and services, becoming a strategic partner for our customers in the hydrocarbon, mining, industrial and energy sectors, through the application of a forward-looking portfolio and new technologies aimed at its development, fostering a positive work environment, equal opportunity, and a positive impact on employees, customers, suppliers, the environment and all stakeholders of the organization.
