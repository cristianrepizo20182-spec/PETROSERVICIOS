From Idea to Machined Drawing: How We Guarantee Precision in Industrial Parts

Aug 27, 20265 min readStandard: ISO 9001
Real photo from the Petroservicios Industriales workshop.

Introduction

In modern industry, a single component failing due to a micrometric tolerance deviation can shut down an entire production line or a drilling rig. Custom manufacturing to drawings requires an integrated methodology that combines digital design with shop-floor precision.

The Manufacturing-to-Drawings Process

  1. Detail Engineering Review: Inspection of dimensional tolerances, material specifications (alloy steels, stainless steels) and surface finish (Ra).
  2. Simulation Software Validation: Stress analysis and machinability review before the first chip is cut.
  3. CNC and Conventional Machining: Turning and milling with high-speed carbide tools to ensure exact geometries.
  4. Metrology Control: Verification with calibrated instruments (micrometers, go/no-go gauges, roughness testers).

The Advantage of Simulation Software

Using simulation software makes it possible to detect mechanical interference and stress concentrations before actual production, optimizing material cost and ensuring the part will hold up under the required working loads.
