Our History

Our story: from a workshop in Carvajal to API and ISO certifications

Oct 4, 20264 min readTopic: PSI history

A workshop in Carvajal

Petroservicios Industriales S.A.S. was born in January 2007, on the initiative of its founders, Cristo Rangel and Arlan Penagos, with the conviction that well-done work, commitment and closeness to customers would be the pillars of a solid company. Operations began in the Carvajal neighborhood of Bogotá, with seven employees dedicated to metalworking jobs for the oil & gas sector.

From those first days we worked for companies such as Parker, Serinco and Saxxon, experiences that gave us technical know-how and a reputation built on reliability and quality.

Growing and gaining precision

Around 2010 we expanded our installed capacity, added new machinery and grew to about 17 employees to meet market demand. Around 2011 we took another step: we acquired CNC machinery (CMM and CNC lathes) and the appropriate measuring equipment.

It was not just about buying machines. It was a strategic decision to increase production, improve precision, meet the technical demands of the Wellhead Control sector and shorten lead times without compromising quality.

Quality as part of our culture

Between mid-2012 and early 2013 we implemented a quality management system that culminated in ISO 9001 certification, with the support of FMC Technologies, today TechnipFMC. That milestone gave a structured form to what we were already practicing: order, reliability, traceability and continuous improvement.

Meeting the standards of the sector

Over the years, the Colombian oil & gas market raised its technical and quality requirements. We understood that we had to demonstrate our capabilities with specific international standards, and API certifications became key for the requirements of large operators. We then began a process of technical preparation and adaptation of our processes.

Moving forward through the pandemic

Right as that process was advancing, the COVID-19 pandemic arrived, a period of uncertainty that tested our ability to adapt. We did not stop: we took part in construction-sector projects, including work related to Hidroituango in Antioquia. The lesson was clear: a company's strength is also proven when conditions are difficult.

PSI today

Today we hold API Q1, API 5B, API 7-1, API 7-2 and API 6A certifications, as well as ISO 9001:2015 and a score of 90 points in RUC. We keep betting on innovation, technology and strengthening our capabilities, with a skilled and trained team.

But what remains from that small workshop in Carvajal is the people: employees who have grown with the organization, customers who have placed their trust in us, and relationships built over time.

And the story continues: the best is yet to be built.
