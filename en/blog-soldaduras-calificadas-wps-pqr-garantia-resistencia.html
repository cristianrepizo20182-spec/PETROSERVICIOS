Qualified WPS and PQR Welding: A Strength Guarantee for Heavy Industry

Aug 27, 20265 min readStandard: ISO 3834 / ASME IX

Introduction

In the industrial and oil & gas sectors, welding is not a simple metal-joining process; it is an engineering discipline that determines the structural safety of bridges, skids, vessels and pressure manifolds. To ensure a welded joint resists without fracturing, working under qualified specifications is mandatory.

Key Documents in Welding Control

  • WPS (Welding Procedure Specification): The detailed "recipe" the welder must follow. It defines variables such as joint type, base metal, filler metal, amperage, voltage, travel speed and preheat temperature.
  • PQR (Procedure Qualification Record): The official record of the destructive tests (tensile, bend, impact) applied to a test coupon to demonstrate that the WPS produces welds with the required mechanical properties.
  • WPQR (Welder Performance Qualification): An individual certificate attesting that the welder has the operational skill to apply a specific WPS.

Supporting Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

To verify quality without damaging the structure, inspections such as the following are applied:

  • Visual Inspection (VT)
  • Industrial Ultrasound (UT)
  • Magnetic Particle Testing (MT)
  • Dye Penetrant Inspection (PT)
