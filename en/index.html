API What is it and why does it matter?

API (American Petroleum Institute) sets the design, manufacturing and threading standards for the equipment that works in a well: valves, wellheads, connections, tubulars and intervention tools. A licensed manufacturer proves, under periodic audit, that every part withstands the pressures and extreme conditions of oil field operations. For an operator it isn't optional: it's what prevents catastrophic well failures and what their own safety standards require.

ISO What is it and why does it matter?

ISO 9001 certifies the quality management system of the entire company — how every order is quoted, manufactured, inspected, documented and delivered — not a single product. It means PSI's processes are audited by an independent third party and reviewed every year to stay valid. It's the standard most companies, oil-related or not, require from their suppliers before qualifying them.

What does this guarantee you as a customer?

  • Full traceability: every part can be traced back to its steel heat and its inspection.
  • Lower risk of field failures and unplanned downtime.
  • You can use our certifications directly in your own supplier audits.
  • You meet the "qualified supplier" requirement in tenders with operators.
  • Verifiable documentation backing every part we manufacture.
  • Annual audits that keep the standard current, not a certificate earned once.
